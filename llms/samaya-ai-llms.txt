# Samaya AI
> Samaya AI is the Expert AI platform built for finance. We bridge frontier AI research with deep understanding of how investment professionals work, enabling a new level of human-AI collaboration that takes investors from information to conviction.

Samaya was founded by AI researchers from Google Brain and Stanford who deeply understand both the frontier of AI and the complexity of global financial markets. Our Expert AI Agents are domain-trained, reliable on long-horizon tasks, and instructable to each investor's unique views.

Samaya excels at first-party data ingestion and retrieval, built on deep ML and finance expertise — enabling firms to bring their own proprietary data into a trusted, high-accuracy AI research environment. Samaya is also multi-surface, meeting investment professionals across the tools and workflows they already use.
